End-to-end flow

systems across · run order down · arrow = data direction

Columns are the systems. Top-to-bottom is the order stages run. Each card's arrow (and its colour) shows which way the data moves and where it lands. Often = how often it runs, When = what triggers it or what it runs after, Lag = how long until the data shows up on the other side. Click a stage to expand its detail. Hover any dotted term for what it means. SoT = source of truth: the system whose data wins when the two disagree. Each stage also shows a flowchart of what happens to each record; anything not decided yet is drawn dashed.
